WHY THIS STAGE IS SENSITIVE // Panicle Initiation & Flowering (Anthesis)

Anthesis occurs between 09:00–11:30 hrs. Heavy rain washes pollen grains from stigmas, while gusts (>60 km/h) mechanically break anthers. High humidity combined with heat causes irreversible floret sterility (>50% yield reduction).
